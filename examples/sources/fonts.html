CSS1 font properties, face by face

Five TrueType programs are supplied as bytes and bound to labelled families by the caller; no system font is consulted. Every short line below is a measured sample of sizes, weights, styles, small capitals, per-character fallback and the font shorthand.

Lato Regular, Lato Bold, Lato Italic, Lato Bold Italic and Noto Sans Regular, each bound alone to a one-face family. The word has a different width in every program, which identifies the face chosen on the later lines.

Small capitals

Lato has no small-capital glyphs: lowercase letters are drawn as capitals at 0.8 of the font size, capitals, digits and punctuation are unchanged and the sharp s becomes SS. In order: lowercase, mixed case, digits, punctuation, sharp s, bold, inherited, and normal restored inside small-caps.

Engraved binds Noto Sans as its small-caps face beside two upright Lato faces. Small-caps text takes that face as written, in bold too because the variant is matched before the weight, and normal text keeps Lato. Noto Sans on its own carries no such label: the last sample is synthesized from its capitals and its OpenType small capitals are not used.

Fallback, character by character

A character missing from the first family is taken from the next one, then the list starts again. "Lato, Noto Sans": the double-struck R and the digamma come from Noto Sans. "Noto Sans, Lato": the arrow, the circle and the square come from Lato, and an arrow keeps its combining accent in Lato although Noto Sans has the accent. An unlisted family ends in the default family, Noto Sans; so does a character that Lato, listed alone, does not have; and an unlisted family asked for bold italic gets the only face of the default family.
